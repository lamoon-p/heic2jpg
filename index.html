HEIC to JPG

Converts on your device. Your photos never leave this browser tab.

    Add photos to enable the quality slider.

    Limitations and how it works
    • JPG is lossy. Even at quality 100 the pixels are re-compressed. There is no lossless path from HEIC to JPG.
    • HDR and 10-bit photos are flattened to 8-bit standard range. Highlight detail beyond SDR is lost.
    • Location (GPS) is removed on purpose. Date, camera, lens and exposure EXIF are kept. Rotation is baked into the pixels.
    • Colour profile (usually Display P3) is copied across only when the source has one. Viewers that ignore profiles will look slightly muted.
    • The size under the slider is an estimate from one photo, scaled by pixel count. The real total appears after converting.
    • Live Photos and bursts: only the main still is converted. Motion, depth and gain-map data are dropped.
    • Memory: converted files are held in this tab until you download. A few hundred 12-megapixel photos may exhaust a phone browser. Use smaller batches.
    • No network. After the page loads it makes no requests at all. Check the Network tab if you like. Decoding uses libheif compiled to WebAssembly, shipped with this page.
